Quantitative Risk Scoring Formula

The platform calculates incident risk deterministically using weighted corporate variables:

Risk Score = min(100, WAsset + WVector + WExposure + WBlastRadius)
  • Asset Criticality ($W_{Asset}$, max 40): Tier 0 Domain Controller = 40, Tier 1 ERP = 25, Tier 2 VPN/SSO = 12, Tier 3 Workstation = 5.
  • Attack Vector ($W_{Vector}$, max 35): Ransomware = 35, BEC / AiTM = 30, VPN Abuse = 28, DDoS = 18, Insider = 15.
  • Financial Exposure ($W_{Exposure}$, max 20): > $1M = 20, > $500k = 14, > $100k = 8, ≤ $100k = 2.
  • Blast Radius ($W_{BlastRadius}$, max 10): min(Systems × 2, 10).
  • Severity Bands: ≥ 80: SEV-1 Critical (<15m SLA), 55-79: SEV-2 High (<30m), 30-54: SEV-3 Medium (<2h), < 30: SEV-4 Low (<8h).
Standards & Forensic References
  • RFC 3227 (Order of Volatility): Evidence acquisition must proceed from most volatile to least volatile:
    1. Registers, cache
    2. Routing table, ARP cache, process table, kernel statistics, RAM
    3. Temporary file systems
    4. Disk storage media
    5. Remote logging and monitoring data
  • ISO/IEC 27037: Governs digital evidence identification, collection, acquisition, and preservation. Ensures evidence can be verified through dual cryptographic hashing without alteration.
  • NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2: Governs the 5-phase incident handling lifecycle: Preparation → Detection & Analysis → Containment, Eradication & Recovery → Post-Incident Activity.
  • GDPR Article 33: Mandates reporting of personal data breaches to regulatory authorities within 72 hours of becoming aware.
